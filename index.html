    For accurate sizes

    1. Coin and object must lie flat on the same surface. Tilted photos are fine now.
    2. For things that stand up (box, cup, bottle) use Add height. Put the square exactly where the object touches the table, then drag the top. Heights need a tilted photo (20–50° is best).
    3. Use the original photo. Cropping or screenshots remove the lens info.
    4. Fill the frame. A bigger coin in the photo gives a better reading.
    5. Under about 45° of tilt is best. Steeper still works but the ± grows.
