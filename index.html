    For accurate sizes

    1. Lay the coin flat, right next to the object, on the same surface.
    2. Hold the phone straight above, not at an angle.
    3. Works for flat things. A tall object looks bigger than it is because its top is closer to the camera.
    4. Fill the frame. A bigger coin in the photo gives a better reading.
